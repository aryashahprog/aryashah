What would outcomes look like if you ran thousands of venture funds with the same strategy? Set the fund size, number of deals, follow-on reserves and follow-on policy, and it shows the spread of net MOIC and IRR, LP cash flows and the J-curve. Written in R with an interactive Shiny app. The results are illustrative, not a forecast.
