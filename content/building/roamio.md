A student event discovery startup. I built a partnership with DoorDash.

### What I learned

- Distribution can come from partners. Working with a company like DoorDash was a way to reach students without building that reach from scratch.
- Students don't need more events; they need help finding the right ones at the right time.
- Getting attention and getting real use are different things, and the second one is what counts.
