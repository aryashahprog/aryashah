---
title: Brain structure and cognition after premature birth
authors: Arya Shah, Dawn Jensen, Vince Calhoun, Jingyu Liu
description: TReNDS Center research on premature birth, cuneus grey matter volume and receptive vocabulary in the ABCD Study.
poster: /research/prematurity-brain-cognition.pdf
---
At the TReNDS Center, I used machine learning and structural MRI data from the ABCD Study to investigate how premature birth relates to brain structure and cognition. We found differences in cuneus grey matter volume and receptive vocabulary performance across gestational groups.
