At the TReNDS Center, I used machine learning and structural MRI data from the ABCD Study to investigate how premature birth relates to brain structure and cognition. Our analysis identified differences in cuneus grey matter volume and receptive vocabulary performance, examining a potential link between brain structure and cognitive outcomes.
