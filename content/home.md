Hey! I’m Arya. Born and raised in Cyprus, served 14 months in the Cypriot army after high school and now study finance and computer science at Georgia Tech.

I’m an Investments and Insights Analyst at Tech Square Ventures and Engage, researching enterprise technology, where startups fit and what businesses will pay for.

Previously, at University Growth Fund, I worked on deals involving Perplexity, Colossal Biosciences and Sardine AI. I also co-founded an events startup that piloted with DoorDash, worked on AI automation for Coca-Cola and researched [brain structure and cognition](/research/prematurity-brain-cognition/) at the TReNDS Center.

In 2020, I created Cyprus’s first augmented reality shopping experience.

I’m interested in the distance between what technology can do and what people will trust it to do. I [write about that here](/theses/).

Always happy to hear from you.
